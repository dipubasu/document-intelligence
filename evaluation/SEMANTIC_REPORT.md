## Limits of this measurement

- The corpus is small and synthetic. With more documents, ranking gets harder.
- The queries and the list of correct files were written by the developer after seeing the supplier and customer names. This is not a blind test, so results are probably better than on real user queries.
- For topic queries, "relevant" is a human judgment from supplier and customer names, so it is subjective.
- Scores depend on the embedding model and on the text built for each invoice. A threshold chosen here may not fit another model or other data.
- Single run, one embedding model, 20 or fewer queries. Do not treat the percentages as stable.
- Only invoices are searched (not single pages), and only invoices with extracted fields or text are indexed.
